7. Deliberately NOT bundled
   -----------------------------------------------------
   No NVIDIA CUDA/Torch/training data or model weights, no MATLAB artifacts, no
   research caches, no Vivado projects, no keys/secrets, no old PC prototype,
   no .venv, no test fixtures required only by development. The packaging
   bundle_evidence.py tool fails a build if forbidden payloads are detected.

Bundled Qt/PySide6 DLL inventory is recorded at build time in
packaging/build-logs/<timestamp>/bundle_evidence.json (file list + SHA256).
Because the main-agent GUI is not merged yet, the exact Qt module subset for the
final SixMic3D.exe must be regenerated from that bundle evidence at release.
